The Project

Project Overview

Prime Med (PRIME) is Egypt's first integrated medical mall, combining specialized clinics and commercial spaces in October Gardens. Developed by NBIG Developments with 13+ years of real estate expertise across Egypt and the UAE.

  • Strategic location surrounded by 35+ residential compounds
  • Unique concept combining healthcare & shopping in one destination
  • 50 medical clinics + pharmacy + 2 analysis labs
  • Radiology center, mini market, café, and fitness center

The Project

Project Overview

CMC 2 is a state-of-the-art medical mall by NBIG Developments — an extension of the successful CMC 1. Located on Ahmed Zewail Road in Hadayek October, directly opposite the City Authority and Petroleum Company.

  • Strategic location on Ahmed Zewail Road, Hadayek October
  • Directly opposite the City Authority and Petroleum Company
  • 90 fully-finished, air-conditioned clinics
  • Advanced facilities: radiology, labs, pharmacy

Smart Technology & App

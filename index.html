About NBIG Developments

At NBIG Developments, we strive to create world-class real estate projects and deliver innovative solutions that enhance quality of life. From smart medical hubs to luxury residential communities, we build projects that stand the test of time.

Founded in 2013, our company has been a leader in Egypt's real estate industry, with a presence in Egypt, UAE, and Saudi Arabia — offering top-tier services and development expertise.

  • 13+ years of proven real estate development experience
  • Projects across Egypt, UAE, and Saudi Arabia
  • Committed to quality, sustainability, and innovation
  • Full after-sales support and flexible payment plans
